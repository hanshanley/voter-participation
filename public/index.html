Our independent method and reliability tests

We use independently acquired public Census and administrative inputs, not NYT model estimates, commercial voter files or candidate preferences. The first historical prototype did not beat a simple baseline. No modeled 2026 demographic turnout rates are released.

The prototype transfers each group’s average log-odds turnout gap from three earlier same-type elections, using only earlier national turnout. Carry-forward uses the group’s last same-type turnout rate. Presidential and midterm elections stay separate.

98 historical tests: candidate average error 4.22 percentage points; carry-forward 4.17. Lower is better. Historical reliability screen: not passed.
